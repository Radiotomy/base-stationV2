# RIAA / IFPI Generative-AI Labeling Policy — BASE Station Implementation

**Source:** "Music Community Introduces New Labeling Program to Distinguish Generative AI in Sound Recordings" — IFPI, RIAA, A2IM, WIN, IMPALA, The Grammys, SAG-AFTRA & Human Artistry Campaign (July 10, 2026).
https://www.riaa.com/music-community-introduces-new-labeling-programto-distinguish-generative-ai-in-sound-recordings/

## The Standard (track-level labels for SOUND RECORDINGS)

### 🤖 AI-Generated (`ai_generated`)
Generative AI was used to generate the entirety or the primary portion of the creative
elements of the recording. Examples:
- Lead vocal performance generated by AI, or
- Key instrumental performance generated by AI, or
- Entirely prompt-generated AI music.

### 🧠 AI-Assisted (`ai_assisted`)
The recording was created substantially by humans and expresses human creativity;
however, generative AI was used for some expressive elements. Humans performed the
lead vocal and primary instruments.

### 👤 Human (`human`)
No generative AI used in the sound recording.

### Explicit scope exclusions (per the standard)
The labels apply ONLY to generative AI use in **sound recordings**. They do **not**
currently cover generative AI used in **lyrics, composition, music videos, or cover art**.
→ BASE Station's lyrics, cover-art, video and visualizer pipelines do NOT trigger a label.

## BASE Station tool → label mapping

| Pipeline / tool | Label | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| generateMusic (Sonic / Producer / Tempolor) | `ai_generated` | Entirely prompt-generated recording |
| generateCoverSong, generateMashup, generate243Masters | `ai_generated` | AI generates/regenerates primary audio |
| synthesizeVoice, createSonicVoice → vocal tracks | `ai_generated` | Lead vocal generated by AI |
| generateHarmonies | `ai_assisted` | AI adds expressive elements to a human recording |
| masterTrack / aiMastering / applyAudioEffects | `ai_assisted`* | AI processing of a human recording (*if source is human) |
| generateStems (separation) | inherits source label | Separation is non-generative |
| User uploads (SubmitTrack) | self-declared by artist | Artist attests at submission |
| Lyrics / cover art / videos | no label | Out of scope per the standard |

## Label inheritance rule
Derived assets (stems, masters, mashups, visualizer audio) inherit the **most AI-intensive**
label in their provenance chain: `ai_generated` > `ai_assisted` > `human`.

## Storage
- `ai_label` field (enum: `ai_generated` | `ai_assisted` | `human`) on:
  `UserAsset`, `TrackSubmission`, `GenerationJob`, `BaseTrackRegistry`.
- Stamped server-side at job creation in `generateMusic`; carried through
  `pollGenerationJob` / `aimusicapiWebhook` completion into job output metadata.
- Phase 4 will embed the label into exported ID3 metadata (`editID3Tags`).
